# Indian Statutory Lexicon Compliance Mapping

This document details the legal mapping between Indian labor legislation and Stage 1 deterministic dictionary rules in Pattern Specular.

## 1. Code on Wages, 2019

### Statutory Provision: Section 3(1)
Prohibits discrimination on grounds of sex or age in matters related to wages, recruitment, and conditions of employment for the same work or work of similar nature.

| Term | Lemma | Category | Legal Rationale | Neutral Alternatives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `young` | `young` | `age` | Excludes qualified experienced applicants without occupational justification. | `motivated`, `enthusiastic`, `driven` |
| `fresh` | `fresh` | `age` | Proxy for early-career/youth age discrimination. | `recent graduate`, `early-career` |
| `energetic` | `energetic` | `age` | Cultural proxy for youth; violates equal opportunity principles. | `proactive`, `self-driven` |
| `manpower` | `manpower` | `gender` | Gender-exclusive terminology implying male workforce composition. | `workforce`, `personnel`, `team members` |

## 2. Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act, 2016

### Statutory Provision: Section 20
Mandates non-discrimination in employment and reasonable accommodation in workplace conditions unless a physical standard is a Genuine Occupational Requirement (GOR).

| Term | Lemma | Category | Legal Rationale | Neutral Alternatives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `physically fit` | `physically fit` | `disability` | Blanket physical capability screening without essential function nexus. | `able to perform essential job functions`, `meets role requirements` |
| `normal` | `normal` | `disability` | Ableist exclusion implying persons with disabilities are defective. | `typical`, `standard`, `expected` |

## 3. Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act, 2019

### Statutory Provision: Section 9
Strictly prohibits unfair discrimination against transgender persons in recruitment, promotion, and employment terms.

| Term | Lemma | Category | Legal Rationale | Neutral Alternatives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `male/female only` | `male/female only` | `gender` | Explicit binary restriction denying equal employment rights. | `all genders welcome`, `open to all candidates` |
