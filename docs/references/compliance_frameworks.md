# Statutory & Regulatory Compliance Frameworks

Pattern Specular evaluates managerial communications across **four sovereign jurisdictions** (India, Singapore, United States, United Kingdom) using statutory legal grounding and genuine occupational requirement exceptions.

---

## 2. Genuine Occupational Requirements & Statutory Defences

Under all four legal regimes, discriminatory language or requirements are unlawful **unless** they qualify as an objective occupational exception:

* **India (RPwD Act §3 & Code on Wages §3)**: Stated physical or technical criteria must be essential, measurable functions of the post. Blanket physical exclusions without role-specific justifications are prohibited.
* **Singapore (TAFEP GDOR)**: **Genuine and Determining Occupational Requirement**. A narrow defense where the job literally cannot function without the specified requirement.
* **United States (Title VII BFOQ)**: **Bona Fide Occupational Qualification**. Reasonably necessary to the normal operation of that particular business or enterprise.
* **United Kingdom (Equality Act §13)**: **Occupational Requirement (OR)**. A proportionate means of achieving a legitimate aim.

| Compliant Phrasing (Objective / Outcome-Based) | Non-Compliant Phrasing (Identity / Proxy Based) |
|---|---|
| "Must be able to safely lift and carry hardware server racks up to 20 kg." | "Must be physically fit male candidate." |
| "Requires fluency in written and spoken Mandarin to support APAC client portfolio." | "Prefer Chinese candidate to match existing team." |
| "Demonstrated track record of delivering 8+ complex Python/FastAPI microservices." | "Looking for young digital native rockstars." |
| "Role requires flexible emergency availability for critical incident on-call shifts." | "Ideal candidate has no family commitments / can work like a bachelor." |

## 3. Statutory Decision Rules

```
Candidate Phrase / Sentence
            │
            ▼
Does the text reference a protected characteristic or systemic proxy?
            │
            ├── NO  ──► ✅ acceptable (Passed)
            │
            └── YES ──► Does it qualify as an objective Occupational Requirement (GDOR/BFOQ)?
                          │
                          ├── YES (Measurable outcome specified) ──► 🟡 acceptable_with_justification (Advisory)
                          │
                          └── NO (Subjective or discriminatory)   ──► 🔴 unacceptable (Flagged Infraction)
```

### Three Standard Verdicts
1. **`acceptable`**: No bias detected; criteria are neutral and performance-focused.
2. **`acceptable_with_justification`**: A protected characteristic or physical ability is referenced, but grounded as an objective, measurable job requirement. Surfaced as an informational verification note.
3. **`unacceptable`**: Discriminatory or proxy-coded language that exposes the organization to statutory liability. Flagged with citation and recommended alternatives.

## 4. Multi-Jurisdictional Training & Benchmark Examples

### 4A. India Jurisdiction (`IN`)

#### Acceptable
* **`IN-JD-OK1`**: *"Candidate must possess strong problem-solving capabilities and experience leading distributed engineering pods across multiple shift handovers."* — Neutral, competency-based.
* **`IN-PW-OK1`**: *"Aditi demonstrated exceptional technical rigor, spearheading the migration to high-throughput streaming architecture with zero production downtime."* — Objective technical impact.

#### Non-Compliant / Flagged
* **`IN-JD-NO1`**: *"Seeking young and energetic candidates with high bandwidth for aggressive sprint cycles."*  
  *(Violates Code on Wages, 2019 equal remuneration principles; age proxy).*
* **`IN-JD-NO2`**: *"Candidate must be completely normal and physically fit without any physical impediments."*  
  *(Violates Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 §3; ableist exclusion).*
* **`IN-JD-NO3`**: *"Requires male/female only candidates for warehouse lead role."*  
  *(Violates Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act, 2019).*

---

### 4B. Singapore Jurisdiction (`SG`)

#### Acceptable
* **`SG-JD-OK1`**: *"Must be able to stand for extended periods during hardware installations and lift test equipment weighing up to 15 kg."* — Objective physical requirement (GDOR).
* **`SG-IF-OK1`**: *"Jason presented clear architecture diagrams and demonstrated deep knowledge of distributed transaction semantics during whiteboard sessions."* — Evidence-based interview evaluation.

#### Non-Compliant / Flagged
* **`SG-JD-NO1`**: *"We want young digital natives who can work like a bachelor. Candidates in their 20s preferred."*  
  *(Violates TAFEP: Age, marital status, and family responsibility proxies).*
* **`SG-PW-NO1`**: *"Mei Ling is an excellent developer but cannot be promoted to Director as she is expecting and will soon take maternity leave."*  
  *(Violates TAFEP pregnancy and gender protection guidelines).*

---

### 4C. United States (`US`) & United Kingdom (`UK`)

#### Acceptable
* **`US-JD-OK1`**: *"Requires 5+ years of demonstrable experience architecting distributed relational databases in PostgreSQL."* — Years of experience criteria, lawful under ADEA.
* **`UK-PW-OK1`**: *"David exhibited measured, analytical crisis management during our Q3 data center failover, ensuring compliance with FCA regulatory reporting."* — Objective performance metric.

#### Non-Compliant / Flagged
* **`US-JD-NO1`**: *"Looking for a cultural fit rockstar with youthful exuberance."*  
  *(Age proxy under ADEA; subjective exclusionary criteria).*
* **`UK-PW-NO1`**: *"Sarah is technically brilliant but comes across as aggressive and sharp; she should adopt a warmer, more nurturing managerial tone."*  
  *(Prescriptive gender stereotyping under Equality Act 2010).*
